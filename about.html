Anna Bouman

is an artist with a cross-disciplinary background who works through different mediums of singing, performance and drawing. Het artistic practice is concerned with the complex intertwining structures between sence and emotions. It leads to alienating and yet strongly realistic expressions of an inner world.In addition to her artistic practice she is working as an auditor for the Dutch cancer screening program. Anna was a nurse for fifteen years, worked and stayed in Spain and in the Netherlands in different fields of healthcare. She is now a student at the Royal Academy of Arts in the Hague.
